Name of Quantlet: GNNXAI_pipeline

Published in:    Do Graph Embeddings Stabilize SHAP? A Controlled Decomposition under Distribution Shift (submitted, Expert Systems with Applications)

Description:     Single end-to-end entry point that regenerates every cache the
                 figure/table Quantlets consume, in a fixed idempotent order:
                 embeddings -> hybrid -> shap -> stability -> calibration ->
                 figures -> tables. Trains the 28 (configuration, split) models
                 (XGBoost/CatBoost tabular baselines, frozen heterogeneous
                 GraphSAGE hybrids at layers 1-2, and the diagonal-scale random /
                 permuted / noise-injected and covariance-matched negative
                 controls), computes interventional TreeExplainer SHAP, runs the
                 Algorithm-1 stability diagnostic (Jensen-Shannon divergence,
                 block bootstrap, block-permutation with BH), and writes the
                 predictive/calibration tables. A thin launcher over
                 tools/run_pipeline.py; supports --only <stages> and
                 --configs <ids>. NOT self-contained: it needs the raw IEEE-CIS
                 and Elliptic datasets and produces the large intermediate caches
                 that are archived externally. The downstream Quantlets each ship
                 their small committed input and run self-contained.

Keywords:        pipeline, reproducibility, orchestration, GraphSAGE,
                 gradient boosting, SHAP, negative control, distribution shift,
                 fraud detection, end-to-end

See also:        Diaconu and Pele 2026, GNN_XAI_Stability collection;
                 all F* and T* Quantlets consume this pipeline's caches

Author:          Delia Diaconu, Daniel Traian Pele

Submitted:       2026-07-13

Datafile:        Raw IEEE-CIS Fraud Detection and Elliptic Bitcoin datasets
                 (public; downloaded via scripts/00_prepare_data.py, Kaggle
                 account required). Intermediate SHAP/embedding caches are large
                 (several hundred MB), git-ignored, and archived externally (see
                 the paper's Data-availability statement).

Output:          The committed figure/table caches under results/tables/ and
                 submission/quantlets/*/ (stability_*, preds_*, calibration_*,
                 single_set_*, null_bands_all.csv) consumed by the F* / T*
                 Quantlets.
