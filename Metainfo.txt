Name of Quantlet: GNN_XAI_Stability

Published in:    Do Graph Embeddings Stabilize SHAP? A Controlled Decomposition under Distribution Shift (submitted, Expert Systems with Applications)

Description: Do Graph Embeddings Stabilize SHAP? A Controlled Decomposition
             under Distribution Shift. A per-feature SHAP attribution-stability
             diagnostic: Jensen-Shannon divergence across random and temporal
             validation regimes, block-bootstrap intervals, a paired
             block-permutation procedure with Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment,
             and a leave-one-out pairwise re-seeded null band (mean ~40% at
             nominal alpha=0.05). Read against that empirical null the
             graph-augmented hybrids do NOT stabilize SHAP above training-RNG
             noise for either XGBoost or CatBoost; nested negative controls
             show a covariance-matched random embedding reproduces and even
             exceeds the hybrid's null-level movement, so no graph-information
             channel beyond embedding covariance is detected. The same
             re-seeded-null discipline retires the study's one apparent
             positive result --- a single-fit CatBoost calibration (ECE)
             reduction that does not survive re-fitting under twenty training
             seeds --- so neither the stability axis nor the calibration axis
             shows a graph effect. A cautionary negative-control audit,
             validated on IEEE-CIS Fraud Detection with heterogeneous
             GraphSAGE embeddings and replicated on the Elliptic Bitcoin
             transaction dataset.
